# Contributing

## No promotional PRs

This repository does not accept advertising PRs. In particular:

- Do not submit a PR to promote your own product, service, or paid/commercial app.
- Self-promotional PRs will be closed without merging, even if the change itself is small.
- Genuine suggestions for open-source or free apps are welcome. If you are affiliated with the app, say so in the Issue/PR.

## What to submit

- Apps that are open-source, or have a genuinely free tier.
- One app per PR, with a clear description and a working link.
